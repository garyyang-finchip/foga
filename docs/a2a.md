# A2A (POST /a2a, /.well-known/agent-card.json)

The fourth entrance (design memo, sections 7 and 9, 3d-2): the Agent2Agent protocol at version 1.0, the JSON-RPC binding only, on the node's HTTP server. Like MCP and the HTTP API it adds no rule of its own: an inbound message becomes work the catalog judges as the caller, and the economics stay on chain.

## The card

`GET /.well-known/agent-card.json` is the AgentCard (A2A 1.0, section 4.4), one of the documents `foga cell docs build` writes from the configuration (docs/documents.md): `name`, `description`, `supportedInterfaces: [{url: <publicUrl>/a2a, protocolBinding: "JSONRPC", protocolVersion: "1.0"}]` (empty until the cell has a public URL — the caller then uses the URL it knows), `provider` (`presence.provider`), `version` (the package's), `capabilities: {streaming: false, pushNotifications: false, extendedAgentCard: false}`, `securitySchemes` (`api-key`: `Bearer`; `wallet-signature`: `FOGA-EIP712`, docs/wallet.md), `defaultInputModes: ["text/plain", "application/json"]`, `defaultOutputModes: ["application/json", "text/plain"]`, and `skills`: the ERC-8338 skills the policy offers (`skills.offered`), each `id` the package name at its version, with two examples — a quote and a tender — as data parts. Not the token list, not a promise of anything the policy would refuse. The card is not signed (JWS stays for later).

## The binding

`POST /a2a` with `Content-Type: application/json` and `A2A-Version: 1.0` (or `?A2A-Version=1.0`); a JSON-RPC 2.0 request whose `method` is `SendMessage`, `GetTask`, `ListTasks` or `CancelTask`. An empty version is 0.3 by the specification and this agent does not speak it: `VersionNotSupportedError` (-32009). `SendStreamingMessage`, `SubscribeToTask` and `GetExtendedAgentCard` are `UnsupportedOperationError` (-32004), the push-notification methods `PushNotificationNotSupportedError` (-32003), anything else `MethodNotFoundError` (-32601); the error codes and their `data` (`google.rpc.ErrorInfo` with `reason`, `domain`, `metadata`) follow sections 5.4 and 9.5. Field names are camelCase and enums are their Protocol Buffer names (`TASK_STATE_COMPLETED`, `ROLE_USER`), as the specification prescribes for every JSON binding. Credentials are the HTTP server's (`Authorization`: nothing, a Bearer key, or a wallet signature); the caller a message comes from is what the node's access policy makes of them.

## Two roads

A message's data part may carry a `foga` object; a message of words is a quote.

**Quote** — `{"foga": {"mode": "quote", "skill": "foga-probe", "input": {…}}}`, or any text. Open to anyone. The task completes at once with a `quote` artifact: the cell's offered skills (name, version, package hash, token id, summary), the terms a tender needs to meet (the acceptance authority — the world's hashlock verifier — the reward floor, the submit and settle windows, the counterparty level), and how to proceed. A skill the cell does not offer is `TASK_STATE_REJECTED` with the list of what it does. Nothing runs and nothing touches the chain.

**Tender** — `{"foga": {"mode": "tender", "tokenId": "23"}}`, naming an ERC-8414 tender that exists, is funded, and has its answer committed to the hashlock verifier (the caller minted it for its own account — `tender_mint_build` gives the calls — or anyone did). The caller needs the act level: an API key the operator issued, or a wallet signature the cell's policy grants act to (with a request-bound envelope). The public is told to quote (`TASK_STATE_REJECTED`, gate `permission`). Without a `tokenId` the task waits (`TASK_STATE_INPUT_REQUIRED`) and a message with the `taskId` continues it. With one, the response is the task in `TASK_STATE_WORKING` and the work goes on after it (section 3.3.3): `tender_precheck`, then `skill_run` with the task document's input, then `tender_submit` — each through `Catalog.invoke` as the A2A caller, each a decision record under the caller's id. The task ends `TASK_STATE_COMPLETED` with a `submission` artifact (token id, result hash, answer hash, submission id, transaction hash, job and decision ids, the operations), `TASK_STATE_REJECTED` when a gate refused (the gate, the reason and the decision id in the status message's data part), or `TASK_STATE_FAILED` when the run failed. Settlement and withdrawal are the node's watchers' duty afterwards, as for every submission; the caller does not pay the cell through A2A and the cell does not pay the caller: the tender's escrow pays the account.

There is no road that does work first and talks about payment after, and no road that mints or funds a tender on the caller's behalf (who pays and who approves would need an authority this version does not define).

## Tasks

A task lives in the cell's store (`a2a.task.<id>` among the facts): visible to the caller that made it and to the operator's view, never to another caller (`GetTask` answers `TaskNotFoundError` rather than reveal it exists). The same `messageId` from the same caller is the same task (section 3.3.1). A message to a terminal task is `UnsupportedOperationError`. `ListTasks` pages one's own tasks (`pageSize` 1–100, `pageToken` as an offset, `status` and `contextId` filters, `includeArtifacts`); `CancelTask` ends a task that has not started working, asks a working one to stop between its steps, and refuses (`TaskNotCancelableError`) once the submission is broadcast or the task is terminal: what reached the chain stays.

## What the memo's v0.2 asked and where it landed

The signature covers the request content (the request-bound envelope hashes method, path and body); the request right and the act right are separate (a grant decides the level; the cell's own rules decide the act); task and operation are layered (one task, the catalog's jobs beneath it, named in the artifact); only existing, funded tenders are taken; duplicates are detected by (caller, messageId); `CancelTask` undoes nothing broadcast; the content type is `application/json`; `ListTasks` is implemented and scoped to the caller; `supportedInterfaces` lists the one binding; the data types are the proto's names and shapes (`src/a2a/types.ts`). Outbound A2A — the node calling other agents — is not in this phase: the node's work comes from the chain.

`test/a2a.test.ts` drives all of it on anvil with a raw JSON-RPC client; `foga quickstart` checks the card and a quote on every run.
