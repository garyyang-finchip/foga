# Changelog

## 0.5.0-alpha.1 — 2026-11-01 (Phase 2c: the genesis on a public chain)

- `foga world deploy` on an attach-mode config attaches the listed contracts and deploys the plan steps the config does not list from the bundle, with W as deployer (`FOGA_DEPLOYER_PK`, or W of the phrase); the world file records them as `deployed by FOGA from the bundle`, the ARD kind registration is skipped on an attached registry, and `foga world attach` warns when an address book is incomplete for a genesis. `worlds/sepolia.json` lists no ERC-8471 contract any more — the upstream deployment is revision 3 and the revision-4 binding module admits only sources declaring its own interface id, so the two cannot be combined; the revision-3 addresses stay under `upstreamNotAttached` — and FOGA deploys the revision-4 stack together with the two eligibility modules upstream never deployed (15 contracts, about 16 M gas). docs/version-lock.md (seams 1–3 updated).
- Twelve keys from one BIP-39 phrase on every chain (`src/genesis/roles.ts`: role i at `m/44'/60'/0'/0/i`; anvil's phrase on anvil; `FOGA_MNEMONIC` / `FOGA_MNEMONIC_FILE` elsewhere; `FOGA_KEY_<ROLE>` overrides one role). `foga keys new` writes a phrase (mode 0600, never overwritten) and prints the role addresses; `foga keys show` lists them from the environment.
- W is the treasury: before every step it tops up each role whose balance has fallen below half its floor (`src/genesis/funding.ts`: a gas budget per role from a full local run, priced at the chain's current fee quote, at least 1 gwei; fixed floors on anvil) and gives each cell its capital at birth (0.05 ETH on anvil, 0.01 ETH elsewhere; the successor 0.01 / 0.002). Amounts are memoized facts (`T<n>.fund.price`, `T<n>.fund.<role>.amount`). `foga genesis preflight --world <world> [--rpc]` prints the plan, what W must hold, the escrow's admin, the backing registry, the proving material and the waits; it sends nothing (exit 5 on a problem).
- A backing ERC-8434 registry: where the world names `8434/AIDRegistry(ard-fixture)` at another address than the primary registry, the cells bind there at birth, heartbeat there in T7, and the takeover and the retirement of T9 are mirrored there (`*.aid.backing.*` operations, checks `X.aid.backing`, `anchor.backing.active`, `Aprime.backing.active`); the ARD `backed` checks read it. `AID.backing`, `AID.at`, `stateName(anchor, registry)`.
- T7 reaches STALE by the anchor's own `setLivenessWindow(120)` and a wait, on every chain, then heartbeats and restores the default window (`T7.A.aid.window`, `T7.A.aid.window.reset`; check `anchor.window.restored`); the 90-day clock skip is gone. `AID.encodeSetLivenessWindow`, `livenessWindow`, `defaultLivenessWindow`.
- Public timing profile shortened within the registries' constraints (judgment of the no-decision tender 600 s; service filing 1800 s, evidence 180 s, decision 600 s, review 120 s, enactment margin 120 s, dispute period 1800 s): about fifty minutes of waiting over T5–T7.
- Kernel: a transaction still pending after the receipt timeout is replaced under the same nonce with fees a third higher, up to three times (`replaced`, `replace-rejected` journal entries; every hash of an operation is recognized on recovery and replay); the provider polls public endpoints every 4 s (anvil 250 ms). Test against anvil with automine off (`test/tx-replace.test.ts`, skipped when anvil is absent). Evidence summary gains `replaced`.
- Scenario version 4 (stores from 0.4 are refused). On anvil: 187 operations (J1 is pre-funded and no longer topped up; the T7 window operations are new), 166 checks. `scripts/attach-rehearsal.mjs` builds an attach-mode config from a deployed local world (second AIDRegistry as backing registry, ERC-8471 and the two eligibility modules left out); CI runs the hybrid deploy, the preflight, the genesis T0–T9 and the doctor on it on a second anvil (171 checks, 195 operations, all eleven standards Pass). `deployments/local-attach*.example.json` are that run.
- Docs: genesis ("Running on a public chain", actors, T0/T1/T7/T9 tables, windows, counts), kernel (replacement, polling, keys and funds), version-lock, doctor, compatibility matrix, README; CLI help (`keys`, `genesis preflight`, environment).

## 0.4.0-alpha.2 — 2026-10-31

- Kernel: a receipt that does not arrive within the timeout is reported in the kernel's own words (the row stays `sent`, the next run recovers it from the chain; check whether another process is saturating the machine) and journaled as `receipt-timeout`, instead of surfacing ethers' TIMEOUT stack trace. Seen on a small EC2 instance where `npm run zk:predicate` ran at the same time as the genesis and starved anvil.
- `foga demo` warns at start when the machine is already busy (load above 80 % of the cores): a proving-key build running alongside slows anvil down until the genesis times out.
- docs/zk.md: run the predicate build alone (all cores, close to 1 GB of memory; about an hour on 2 cores, 35 minutes on GitHub's runners); the committed verifier was reproduced byte for byte by CI on a cache miss.

## 0.4.0-alpha.1 — 2026-10-31 (Phase 2b)

- The predicate-pattern ZK path of ERC-8419: `zk/circuits/kya_predicate_count_v1.circom` (8 record slots, source set of depth 16, EdDSA-Poseidon records, nullifier scoped to scheme, admission domain, epoch and challenge); `contracts/src/zk/Groth16PredicateKYAVerifierAdapter.sol` (IKYAVerifier for the `kya-predicate-count-v1` layout: result 1 → level 1, a false predicate refused, claim digest over the rule, source-set root as anchor, epoch window; 9 Foundry tests) and the generated `Groth16PredicateVerifier.sol`; `prover/build-predicate.mjs`, a deterministic development ceremony with a public beacon (`npm run zk:predicate`); `prover/run.cjs` gains `record.sign` and `predicate.prove`; T0 registers `S_pred` with FOGA's adapter pinned to a source set held by W; T3 ends with A's proof over its first accepted completion and six negatives (replay, subject, scheme, K above the count, flipped result bit, stale epoch). `--zk auto|require|skip` now covers both paths (`T0.zk.predicate`). docs/zk.md.
- T0 registers the ADRF policy FOGA-Service-v1 (formal: `DeclaredVersionVerifier`; judged: the `ADRFACDFBridge` with FOGA-Council; one effect, the refund) and binds the world's `ADRFServiceEscrow` to it as its admin, W. `src/core/adrf.ts`.
- T6 (dispute): deposit, call committed to v1, skill v2 published and delivered, charge held; filing by the client (stranger and self-filing refused, duplicates refused), formal finding TRUE, judged finding FALSE through the bridge with provenance, decision, review window, Final Sustained, the refund enacted once by the escrow and reported; the conforming control call released to the provider.
- T7 (safety): a two-completion tender; the guardian's suspension; the kernel's recorded decision; a raw transaction around the kernel failing on chain at the gate; a de-risk withdrawal (new allow-list entry `ADRFServiceEscrow.withdrawBalance`, T1) passing under it; the operator's direct, unattributable submission and payout; a fresh binding refused by the standing stop; the adopter unable to lift the intervener's suspension; resume, submission, payment; the STALE gate after the liveness window and the heartbeats.
- T8 (measurement): `src/core/measure.ts` — the `foga.realized-net-cash-flow/1` method descriptor, observation receipts (settled inflows inside the boundary, gas as cost, missing items, no value field) and a deterministic resolver (method registered, unit version, boundary, totals, double counting across receipts, the on-chain assertion); E registers the method and asserts receipts for cell A (T3–T7) and the swarm (T4–T5); the double-count and unregistered-method cases fail as they should. Unit tests.
- T9 (succession): A-prime deployed and configured, its own binding adopted, the ERC-8004 agent transferred through A's account, A STALE, A-prime's takeover bind, A's retirement with successor, A-prime's new ARD record and document, the skill token moved and its record adopted again by recommitting the same descriptor, the non-inheritance checks (standing, swarm seat, unit, binding, the retired anchor's gate).
- Doctor layer 5: `foga doctor --genesis <evidence.json>` (`src/doctor/semantic.ts`) attributes the genesis checks to the standards they exercise and reports per ERC; the demo runs it after the genesis. Unit tests.
- Kernel and core: `kernel.adrf`, `units` assertion-registry helpers, `kya.getAssertion`, `ard.encodeRecommit`, `wouldRevert` as the owner; scenario version 3 (stores from 0.3 are refused); `--until` and the demo default to T9; CI runs T0–T9 with both ZK paths required, caches the predicate build by circuit hash and checks the committed verifier against a rebuild.
- `artifacts/bundle.json` rebuilt with the two new contracts (163 contracts; hash `0x29340bc3…`).
- 186 operations and 165 checks on anvil (about 95 s, five Groth16 proofs); a replayed run records the checks that later steps supersede as skipped (`check(..., until)`). Docs: genesis (T6–T9, S_pred, windows), zk, doctor, kernel, cell account, compatibility matrix, README.

## 0.2.0-alpha.4 — 2026-10-31

- `foga demo --keep` returns once the run is done and leaves anvil running detached (it printed the pid before but kept the process alive until anvil exited). anvil's output goes to `.foga-build/anvil.log` instead of a pipe, so a kept node cannot die on a closed stderr; the startup diagnostics read that file. ## 0.2.0-alpha.3 — 2026-10-30

- `scripts/install-foundry.sh`: installs the statically linked (musl) build of the pinned Foundry release for Linux machines whose glibc is older than 2.35 (Amazon Linux 2/2023, CentOS 7, Debian 11), verifying the release checksum. `FOGA_FOUNDRY_DIR` makes `foga demo` start anvil from that directory; the demo recognizes the `GLIBC_x.y not found` failure and points at the script.
